Extract page ranges

Enter the pages you want. They will be combined into one new PDF in the order entered.

Split every page

Create one PDF per page and package all the files into a ZIP download.

Privacy: splitting happens locally in your browser with PDF-Lib. ZIP creation also happens in-browser. Your selected PDF is not sent to One T Media Group for processing. Password-protected or damaged PDFs may not open.

Page range examples

Use commas to combine individual pages and ranges:

1-3, 6, 9-12

This extracts pages 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 10, 11 and 12 into one new PDF.

Two ways to split

Extract Pages is useful when you only need part of a document. Split All Pages creates a separate one-page PDF for every page and bundles them into a ZIP so you do not have to download them individually.
