Chomp Champs Privacy Policy

One T Media Group respects your privacy. This policy explains how Chomp Champs handles information when you use the game.

Information stored on your device

Chomp Champs stores gameplay information such as preferences, selected avatar, profile settings, progress, statistics, and achievement status on your device. One T Media Group does not use this information to identify you or sell it to third parties.

Game Center and online play

Chomp Champs uses Apple Game Center for authentication, multiplayer matchmaking, leaderboards, achievements, and player identity during online matches. Information provided through Game Center is handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy and your Game Center settings. Other players may see your Game Center display name, avatar or game profile avatar, rank, match score, and gameplay activity needed to run the match.

Chomp Buddies

If you choose to save another player as a Chomp Buddy, the game stores the Game Center player identifier needed to recognize that player and support future game requests. Chomp Champs does not include chat or private messaging.

Purchases

If in-app purchases are offered, transactions are processed by Apple. One T Media Group does not receive or store your payment card details. The game may receive purchase status information needed to unlock purchased content or restore purchases.

Diagnostics

Apple may provide aggregated or diagnostic information, such as crash reports and performance data, according to your device and App Store privacy settings. We use this information only to maintain and improve the game.

Children’s privacy

Chomp Champs does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Online and purchasing features are provided through Apple services and device-level parental controls.

Data sharing and advertising

One T Media Group does not sell personal information. Chomp Champs does not use third-party advertising trackers or cross-app tracking.

Retention and deletion

Locally stored game data remains on your device until it is cleared by the app, you reset your progress, or you remove the app. Game Center records are controlled through Apple’s services. For privacy questions or deletion requests concerning data held directly by One T Media Group, contact us below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.

Contact

For privacy questions about Chomp Champs, email contact@onetmediagroup.ca.
