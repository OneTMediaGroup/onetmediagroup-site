Your account and your connections.

7D is an early version operated by One T Media Group. Accounts and connections are real; AI chat and memory search are not connected.

What is stored

Firebase Authentication handles your email address, password authentication, verification, and password recovery. 7D stores your display name, optional short bio, chosen colour, account identifier, and profile timestamps in a separate 7D Firebase project. Profile and invitation data use a Firestore database in Toronto; authentication and service processing may occur elsewhere.

Who can see your profile

You and the people you have confirmed a connection with can view your name, bio, and colour. There is no public profile directory. Your email is not included in your shared profile. Project administrators have technical access for support and operation.

Invitations and removal

An invitation stores the sender’s account identifier and display name, the intended recipient’s email, the chosen relationship, status, and timestamps. Only the sender and the verified account using the recipient’s email can read it. The recipient can accept or decline; the sender can cancel. Pending invitations expire after seven days. Expired and completed invitation records are retained until removed through an account-data request.

Accepting adds the connection for both people. Either person can remove it, which revokes future profile access for both. Information already seen or copied by someone cannot be recalled.

Your browser

Sign-in is kept for the browser session. Theme preferences are saved on your device. This account interface does not load analytics, advertising, or AI services, and it does not upload photos or memories.

Access, correction, or deletion

Edit your profile in your universe. To request account deletion or a copy of your account data, contact One T Media Group. Requests require identity verification. Self-service account deletion, photos, pets, circles, and memory sharing are future work.
