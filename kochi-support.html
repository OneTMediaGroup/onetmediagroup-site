KOCHI — A Game of Three

Support

Need a hand with the board, progression, customization or a technical issue? Start here. If something still is not working, you can contact One T Media Group directly.

How to Play

Each player controls three pieces on a nine-point board.

  • Players first take turns placing their three pieces on empty points.
  • Once all six pieces are placed, the movement phase begins.
  • Move one of your pieces to an adjacent empty point along exactly one drawn connection.
  • Pieces cannot jump or travel across more than one connection in a turn.
  • Form a horizontal, vertical or diagonal line with all three of your pieces to win, during placement or movement.

AI Rivals & Journey

KOCHI includes personality-driven AI rivals with different approaches to the board. Playing AI matches builds your journey and opens additional challenges as you progress.

If an opponent or challenge is locked, check the requirement shown in the Journey screen. Some content is meant to be discovered through continued play.

Local Two-Player

Pass & Play lets two people play KOCHI locally on the same device. Players take turns placing and moving their pieces using the same standard KOCHI rules.

No online connection is required for a local Pass & Play match.

Online Play & Rankings

Play 1 vs 1 using the same nine-point rules. Casual matches are untimed. Ranked matches give each player 60 seconds for the whole match; your clock continues on your turn if you disconnect, and running out of time loses the match.

Only ranked results change your rating. Compare world, country and friends rankings. Rematches are casual, require both players to accept, and swap colours.

Each player needs a separate KOCHI account. Two phones signed into the same account cannot play against each other, even if the displayed names look different. Separate accounts can use the same display name.

If a match does not connect, check your internet connection and which account each phone is using.

Themes, Pieces & Customization

KOCHI includes unlockable backgrounds, boards, playing pieces, line styles and profile avatars. Use the Collection area to view available items and see what is still locked.

Unlock requirements are displayed in the game so you can see what to work toward next.

Remove Ads & Restore Purchases

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase that removes automatic ads. It is not a subscription. Optional videos for bonus XP remain available if you choose to watch them.

Open Remove Ads on the home screen to see your local price and buy. To recover an existing purchase, choose Restore Purchases on that screen, or open Settings → Purchases → Restore Purchases.

Use the same Apple Account for App Store purchases that bought Remove Ads. A KOCHI account is not required to restore it. If the price is unavailable, check your connection and try again later. For purchase help, contact support; do not send payment-card details or passwords.

Sign In & Play on Another Phone

KOCHI supports Apple, Google, and email with a password. On another iPhone, sign in to the same KOCHI account using a method already connected to it. Google or email sign-in does not require changing the phone's Apple Account.

To keep your existing KOCHI identity, connect a sign-in method from your current profile. Creating a separate account does not automatically merge progress or rankings. Use the email password-reset option if you forget your KOCHI password.

Save & Restore Your Journey

Open Online → Profile and find Journey backup. Save this phone's journey to keep its progress in the cloud, or use the cloud journey to restore an existing backup. Check the displayed XP and games before choosing which journey to keep.

Use Sync journey to sync your progress while signed in. On another phone, sign in to the same KOCHI account to access that backup. An unfinished local match stays on the original device.

Delete Your KOCHI Account

Open Online → Profile → Account settings → Delete account and follow the confirmation steps. You may need to sign in again to confirm ownership.

Deletion removes your online profile, rating, friends and cloud journey. Shared match records are retained with your account identifier replaced. Local progress on your phone is separate. Deleting a KOCHI account does not cancel or refund an Apple purchase.

See the privacy policy for retention details, or contact support if you cannot access your account.

Rewarded Ads

Rewarded ads are optional. When an eligible reward is offered, you can choose to watch an ad for the displayed bonus or continue playing normally without it.

If an ad does not load, check your internet connection and try again later. Ad availability can vary by region, device and network conditions.

Sound & Character Audio

KOCHI uses character dialogue and game audio during play. Check the in-game settings if you want to adjust available audio options.

If audio unexpectedly stops, confirm your device volume and audio settings, then fully close and reopen KOCHI.

Progress or Unlock Not Updating

First return to the main game screen and reopen the Journey or Collection area. If the displayed progress still has not updated, fully close KOCHI and launch it again.

When contacting support about missing progress, include the challenge or item name and what happened immediately before the issue appeared.

Troubleshooting

If KOCHI is not behaving as expected, try these basic steps first:

  • Fully close and reopen the app.
  • Confirm your device is running a supported version of iOS.
  • Check your internet connection for features that require online access.
  • Install the latest KOCHI update when one is available.

Report a Problem

When reporting a problem, including a few details helps us reproduce it faster:

  • Your iPhone or iPad model.
  • Your iOS version.
  • Your KOCHI app version.
  • What you were doing when the problem occurred.
  • A screenshot, if it helps show the issue.
