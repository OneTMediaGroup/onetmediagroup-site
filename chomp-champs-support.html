Chomp Champs Support

Need help with a match, Game Center, sound, progress, or a purchase? Start with the quick checks below or contact One T Media Group directly.

Online matches

  • Confirm you are signed in to Game Center in iOS Settings.
  • Use a stable internet connection and make sure every player is on the current app version.
  • If matchmaking stalls, leave the match screen and begin a fresh search.

Sound and announcements

  • Check the in-game music, sound-effect, and announcer settings.
  • Confirm the device is not muted and its media volume is raised.
  • Bluetooth audio may continue through a connected headset or speaker.

Achievements and leaderboards

Game Center must be signed in before achievements and leaderboard results can update. Allow a short time for Apple’s service to synchronize recent progress.

Purchases and restores

Purchases are processed by Apple. Use the game’s Restore Purchases option with the same Apple Account originally used to buy the content.

Report a problem

Please include your device model, iOS version, Chomp Champs version, game mode, number of players, and what happened. A screenshot or short recording is extremely helpful.

Privacy

Read the full Chomp Champs Privacy Policy for details about local game data, Game Center, online play, and purchases.

Still need help?

Email our support team and use “Chomp Champs Support” in the subject line.
