One T Tools privacy information. Updated October 7, 2026.

Local processing

The tools process selected files and entered text on your device. Files, invoice details, generated passwords and pasted text are not uploaded to One T Media Group for processing. Output downloads are created locally. Closing the page clears its in-memory work; downloaded files and copied clipboard contents remain under your control.

Optional Google Analytics

Google Analytics 4 is currently inactive while account configuration is pending. When configured, it loads only if you allow analytics. It can receive page visits and fixed tool action names, plus normal connection and device information such as IP address and browser characteristics. Our event code excludes file names, input text, passwords, invoice details, URL query strings and fragments. Analytics can use cookies after acceptance. The analytics preference is stored in this browser for up to 180 days. Use Analytics settings in the footer to change your choice.

Advertising

AdSense is currently inactive while account and consent configuration are pending. When enabled, advertising providers may process connection information and use cookies or similar technologies in accordance with your applicable consent choices. AdSense placements are labelled Advertisement and placed below tool content. The analytics preference alone does not grant advertising consent.

Third-party libraries

Some tools download libraries from jsDelivr. The background remover also downloads AI model assets. Asset hosts receive the normal connection information needed to deliver those files. Loading those assets does not send your selected image or PDF to One T for processing.

Browser preferences and controls

All tools work without analytics or advertising consent. Browser permissions may affect clipboard access or downloads. You can clear site storage in your browser to remove the stored analytics preference.

Google information

See Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses data in its business products.

Open-source software

The QR generator uses qrcode 1.4.4 under the MIT licence. HEIC conversion uses heic-to under LGPL-3.0. Background removal uses IMG.LY background-removal under AGPL-3.0, or a separate commercial licence where applicable. Other third-party notices are listed in the library notices. The tool page source can be viewed in your browser.
