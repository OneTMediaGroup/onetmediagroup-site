Longer is stronger

For randomly generated passwords, increasing length is one of the simplest ways to increase resistance to guessing. A password manager can make long, unique passwords practical because you do not need to memorize every one.

Use a unique password

Do not reuse an important password across multiple services. If one service is breached, password reuse can expose accounts elsewhere. For accounts that support it, multi-factor authentication adds another layer of protection.
