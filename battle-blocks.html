Battle Blocks

Shoot. Block. Outlast.

Break enemy formations, drop defensive blockers, and grab power-ups in a neon arcade arena.

Choose your battle

  • Survival — outlast advancing formations and take on a boss every five waves.
  • Solo and same-phone play — face the CPU, share a phone for Local Versus, or take turns uncovering hidden blocks in Block Hunt.
  • Online Versus — climb the weekly Quick Match leaderboard or invite a friend to a best-of-three series.

All modes are free. The optional Cosmetic Theme Pack adds Deep Space and Pixel Cabinet.
