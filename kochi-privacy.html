KOCHI — A Game of Three

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how KOCHI — A Game of Three, published by One T Media Group, handles information when you use the app.

Last updated: September 26, 2026

1. Overview

KOCHI — A Game of Three is published by One T Media Group in Ontario, Canada. This policy describes information used to run the game, provide online accounts and matches, save progress, handle purchases and advertising, and respond to support requests. Local play does not require a KOCHI account.

2. Accounts and Sign-In

Online features use a Firebase account identifier, including when you start as a guest. If you choose Apple, Google or email sign-in, Firebase Authentication processes the sign-in credentials and account information needed to authenticate you. This includes your account identifier, linked sign-in providers and email address supplied by you or the provider. Apple may supply a private relay email address if you choose Hide My Email.

Email passwords are handled through Firebase Authentication. Your password is not part of your public player profile. Authentication services also process technical information such as IP addresses and user-agent information for security and account management.

3. Profiles, Friends and Online Matches

We store your chosen display name, built-in avatar and optional country, along with ratings, match moves and results, matchmaking requests, and friend requests and relationships. We use these records to connect players, validate turns, calculate rankings and provide friends and rematch features.

Your display name, avatar, selected country and competitive information can be visible to other players through matches, rankings and friends features. Your account email is not displayed as part of your public player profile. Country is optional and selected by you; it is not a request for your precise location.

4. Progress and Cloud Journey

Game progress, XP, unlocks, settings, selected cosmetics and local game state are stored on your device. When you use journey backup and sync, progress is also stored privately in Firebase under your KOCHI account so you can restore it on another device. An unfinished local match stays on the original device.

5. Purchases

Apple handles payments for the one-time Remove Ads purchase. KOCHI uses StoreKit purchase and transaction information to verify ownership and restore purchases. We do not receive your payment-card details. Remove Ads disables automatic ads; optional videos for bonus XP remain available when you choose to watch them.

Purchase ownership uses the Apple Account that made the purchase and is separate from your KOCHI account. Deleting a KOCHI account does not refund an Apple purchase.

6. Advertising and Choices

KOCHI uses Google AdMob for automatic and optional rewarded ads, with Google's consent tools to present applicable choices. The app requests non-personalized ads and disables AdMob's publisher first-party identifier. Non-personalized ads still involve data processing for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention.

Google's advertising services can process IP addresses, approximate location inferred from those addresses, device and app identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostic information. Collection depends on the SDK, device settings and consent choices. See Google's advertising SDK data disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Rewarded videos are optional; you can continue playing without watching them. Where available, use the app's advertising privacy options to review your consent choices. KOCHI's current configuration does not request Apple's permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites.

7. Security and Technical Information

Firebase App Check and Apple's device-attestation services help check that requests come from the app. These services process integrity checks and security tokens. Backend services also process technical and operational information, including request and error logs, to operate online play and investigate abuse or failures.

Apple and service providers may process performance and crash information through their services and your device settings. We use access controls and other technical measures to protect information, but no electronic system can guarantee complete security.

8. Service Providers and Processing Locations

KOCHI uses Google Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions and App Check for accounts, online play, storage and security; Google Sign-In and Sign in with Apple for optional account access; Google AdMob and its consent tools for advertising; and Apple StoreKit for purchases.

These providers process information to deliver their services. Information may be processed outside your province or country, including in the United States, where our online backend is hosted. Provider handling is described in Firebase's privacy and security information, Google's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy.

9. Account Deletion and Retention

Request deletion in Online → Profile → Account settings → Delete account. You may be asked to authenticate again. Deletion removes your authentication account, online profile, rating, friends and requests, and cloud journey. Some cleanup runs in the background rather than completing immediately.

Shared match records are retained with your account identifier replaced so that other players' match records can remain. A hashed deletion marker is retained to prevent old credentials from recreating the deleted account. Provider backups and security logs follow the providers' retention schedules and may not disappear immediately.

Local progress and recovery copies on your phone are separate from online account deletion. Support correspondence and operational records are retained as needed for support, security, recordkeeping and applicable legal obligations. Contact us if you need help requesting deletion or understanding retained information.

10. Support

If you contact us, we receive your email address, message and any device details or screenshots you include. We use these to respond and investigate the issue. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or unnecessary sensitive information.

11. Children and Families

Local play can be used without creating an account. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of online accounts, advertising and purchases and use the controls available on their devices. If you believe a child has provided personal information that should be removed, contact us so we can investigate and assist.

12. Your Choices

You can change your player profile, leave your country unset, choose whether to use cloud journey backup, decline optional rewarded videos, restore purchases and request account deletion. For access, correction or other privacy requests, contact us below. We may need to verify account ownership before acting on a request.

13. Policy Updates

We update this page when the app or its information practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.

14. Contact

For KOCHI privacy questions, email contact@onetmediagroup.ca.
