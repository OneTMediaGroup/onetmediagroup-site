KOCHI — A Game of Three

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how KOCHI — A Game of Three, published by One T Media Group, handles information when you use the app.

Last updated: September 24, 2026

1. Overview

KOCHI is an iOS strategy game published by One T Media Group. We aim to keep data collection limited to what is reasonably needed to operate, support and improve the app, provide optional advertising features, and meet platform and legal requirements.

2. Information You Provide

You may provide information directly to One T Media Group if you contact us for support. This can include your email address, the contents of your message, device or app details you choose to provide, and screenshots or other information you attach to help us investigate a problem.

Support information is used to respond to your request, troubleshoot issues and improve KOCHI. Please do not send sensitive personal information that is not necessary for your support request.

3. Game Progress and Preferences

KOCHI may store game progress, settings, unlocked content, customization choices, statistics and similar gameplay information needed to provide the game's features. Depending on the final feature configuration, some information may be stored locally on your device and some features may use platform or backend services.

4. Advertising and Rewarded Ads

KOCHI may display advertising, including optional rewarded ads. Advertising services can receive technical information needed to request, deliver, measure and protect ads. Depending on your device settings, region, consent choices and the advertising service, this information can include device information, IP address, app interaction data, approximate location derived from network information, and advertising or similar identifiers.

Rewarded advertising is optional. Choosing not to watch a rewarded ad does not prevent you from playing KOCHI's core game.

KOCHI is being prepared to use Google AdMob for advertising. The exact advertising and consent configuration may be updated before release so that this policy matches the production version of the app.

5. Tracking and Advertising Choices

Where required by Apple or applicable law, KOCHI will request any required permission before using data for tracking across apps or websites owned by other companies. You can manage applicable tracking permissions through your iOS privacy settings.

Advertising consent choices may also be presented where required based on your region. Available controls can vary by country, platform version and advertising configuration.

6. Analytics, Diagnostics and Crash Information

KOCHI may use platform or service-provider diagnostics, analytics or crash reporting to understand technical problems, app performance and feature reliability. If these services are enabled in the production app, they may process technical information such as device type, operating system version, app version, crash logs and app interaction data.

We use this type of information for operating, securing, troubleshooting and improving KOCHI rather than to identify you personally.

7. Apple and Platform Services

KOCHI is distributed through Apple's App Store and runs on Apple devices. Apple may process information independently when you download, purchase, update or use apps and platform services. Apple's handling of that information is governed by Apple's own privacy policies and account settings.

8. Third-Party Services

KOCHI may rely on third-party services for functions such as advertising, consent management, diagnostics, analytics, cloud or platform functionality. Those providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable agreements.

The final production list of third-party services should reflect the services actually enabled in the released version of KOCHI.

9. Children's Privacy

KOCHI is not designed to ask children to submit personal information directly to One T Media Group. Advertising and other third-party services, if enabled, will be configured in accordance with the app's intended audience, App Store requirements and applicable privacy obligations.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through a support request or another direct channel, contact us so we can review the matter.

10. Data Retention

Information sent directly to One T Media Group, such as support correspondence, is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for support, troubleshooting, recordkeeping, security and legal purposes. Information handled by third-party services is subject to the retention practices of those providers.

11. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to the information we handle. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have privacy rights relating to personal information, such as requesting access, correction or deletion of information we hold about you. You may also have choices available through iOS, Apple, advertising consent controls and other service-provider settings.

To ask about information you have provided directly to One T Media Group, contact us using the address below.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when KOCHI changes, when third-party services change, or when legal or platform requirements change. The current version will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

14. Contact

Questions about KOCHI privacy can be sent to contact@onetmediagroup.ca.
