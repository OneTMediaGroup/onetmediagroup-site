Turn PDF pages.
The right way up.

Rotate every page or only the pages that need fixing. Turn them left, right or completely upside down — without uploading your PDF.

● PDF processed on your device
Privacy: page rotation happens locally in your browser with PDF-Lib. Your PDF is not sent to One T Media Group for processing. Password-protected or damaged PDFs may not open.

Rotate all or selected pages

Leave Rotate all pages checked to turn the whole document. Uncheck it and enter specific pages or ranges:

2, 4-7, 11

That rotates pages 2, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 11 while leaving every other page alone.

Rotation is additive

The tool respects each page's current rotation and adds your selected turn. So a page already rotated 90° can be turned another 90° rather than having its existing orientation discarded.
