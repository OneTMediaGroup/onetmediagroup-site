Everyday conversions

Switch categories at the top, choose the units and type a value. The result updates immediately. Use the swap button when you want to reverse the conversion.

Fuel economy is backwards

L/100 km measures fuel used per distance, while MPG measures distance per fuel used. That means the relationship is reciprocal rather than a simple multiplier. This converter handles that automatically.
