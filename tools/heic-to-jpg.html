How to convert HEIC to JPG

  1. Choose your HEIC photos. Select one file or a batch.
  2. Choose JPG quality. 92% is a good starting point.
  3. Convert and download. Your browser creates the JPG files locally.

Why convert HEIC?

HEIC is commonly used for efficient photo storage, especially on Apple devices. JPG remains widely supported across websites, older software, email workflows and devices.

HEIC to JPG FAQ

Are my photos uploaded?

No photo upload is required for conversion. Processing is designed to happen on your device.

Can I convert several photos?

Yes. Select multiple HEIC or HEIF files and choose Convert All.

Is metadata preserved?

This tool focuses on image conversion and does not promise to preserve original HEIC metadata in the JPG output.

What quality should I use?

92% is the default. Lower settings reduce file size; higher settings prioritize image quality.
