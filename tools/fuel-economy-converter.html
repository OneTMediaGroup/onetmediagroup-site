Lower L/100 km is better

In L/100 km, a lower number means the vehicle uses less fuel. In MPG and km/L, a higher number means better fuel economy. That's why converting between them uses a reciprocal formula.

Trip fuel estimate

The trip calculator multiplies distance by the converted L/100 km rate. Fuel cost is simply estimated litres × the price per litre you enter; it does not attempt to predict changing fuel prices or driving conditions.
