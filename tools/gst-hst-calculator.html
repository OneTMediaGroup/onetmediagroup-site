GST/HST rates

The current federal/HST rates used here are 13% HST in Ontario, 14% HST in Nova Scotia, 15% HST in New Brunswick, Newfoundland and Labrador, and Prince Edward Island, and 5% GST in non-participating provinces and territories.

PST and QST

For a general retail estimate, this calculator also shows the provincial rate listed by CRA for British Columbia (7%), Manitoba (7%), Saskatchewan (6%) and Quebec QST (9.975%). Provincial tax rules can differ by product, service and circumstance.
