Hourly to annual

Annual gross pay is calculated as hourly rate × hours per week × paid weeks per year. A $30/hour rate at 40 hours per week for 52 paid weeks equals $62,400 gross annually.

Salary to hourly

The reverse calculation divides annual salary by the total working hours you enter. This is useful when comparing a salaried offer with an hourly job using the same work schedule.
