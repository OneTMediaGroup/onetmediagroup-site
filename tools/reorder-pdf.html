Put PDF pages
back in order.

Choose the exact page sequence you want, reverse the whole document or make quick page moves — then download a newly ordered PDF.

● PDF processed on your device
Privacy: page reordering happens locally in your browser with PDF-Lib. Your PDF is not sent to One T Media Group. This tool creates a new PDF containing the pages in the order you specify.

Exact page order

Type the pages in the sequence you want:

3, 1, 2, 4-7

The new PDF starts with page 3, then page 1, page 2, followed by pages 4 through 7. You can omit pages you do not want, or even repeat a page if needed.

Quick page controls

The page chips below the order field let you nudge individual pages left or right. For a long document, editing the order field directly is usually much faster. Reverse All instantly flips the entire document order.
