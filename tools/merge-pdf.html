PDFs

Privacy: PDF merging happens in your browser using the PDF-Lib browser library. Your selected documents are not sent to One T Media Group for merging. Password-protected or damaged PDFs may not be mergeable.

How to merge PDFs

  1. Add the PDF files you want to combine.
  2. Use the arrows to arrange them in the correct order.
  3. Choose a filename.
  4. Merge and download one finished PDF.

What gets merged?

Every page from the first PDF is copied into the new document, followed by every page from the second, then the third, and so on. Reordering the files changes the order of those groups of pages.

This version does not upload documents, edit their visible content or attempt to bypass PDF passwords.
